Sam Gunn

I’m a C.L.E. Moore Postdoc in the Math department at MIT. I did my Ph.D. at UC Berkeley, where my advisor was Umesh Vazirani. My undergrad was at UT Austin, where I had mentorship from Scott Aaronson.

I’m interested in designing algorithms that come with provable guarantees for studying AI. I’m also interested in quantum computing, especially unclonable cryptography.
