Sam Gunn

I’m a C.L.E. Moore Postdoc in the Math department at MIT. I did my Ph.D. at UC Berkeley, where my advisor was Umesh Vazirani. My undergrad was at UT Austin, where I had mentorship from Scott Aaronson. I’m interested in designing algorithms that come with provable guarantees for studying real-world AI. These can be used both for applications in AI safety, and as testbeds for learning about the foundations of AI.
